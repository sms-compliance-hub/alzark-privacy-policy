## Introduction

Alzheimer's Arkansas is an independent, nonprofit organization. We offer free 
programs, services, resources, and financial assistance to family caregivers 
across the state of Arkansas. Our focus is to support the journey our caregivers 
face every day as they care for loved ones with dementia or chronic illness. 
This policy is to further protect information provided by our caregivers, 
sponsors, and friends.

---

## Types of Information Collected

We may collect the following types of information:

- Name
- Email address
- Mailing address
- Phone number

---

## Method of Information Collected

Alzheimer's Arkansas may use information you voluntarily provide on:

- Grant applications
- Volunteer or participant forms
- Sponsorship or donation forms

---

## Purpose of Data Collection

Information collected is used for the following purposes:

- To decide eligibility, tracking, and reporting statistics as may be required
- Communicating basic information about programs, services, or resources available
- For fund-raising activities

---

## SMS / Text Messaging Privacy Disclosure

Mobile opt-in, SMS consent, and phone numbers collected for SMS communication 
purposes will **not** be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

By opting in to receive SMS messages from **Alzheimer's Arkansas**, you agree to 
receive conversational messages related to grants, upcoming events, programs, 
and organizational updates.

- **Message frequency:** Message frequency may vary. On average, 1–2 messages per month.
- **Opt out:** Reply **STOP** to cancel at any time.
- **Help:** Reply **HELP** for assistance, or visit alzark.org
- **Message and data rates:** Message and data rates may apply.

---

## Additional Circumstances Under Which Your Information May Be Used

Your personal information may also be used:

- When there are risks to public health
- In connection with judicial and administrative proceedings
- For law enforcement purposes
- To avert a serious threat to health or safety
- When legally required

---

## Data Security Measures

Alzheimer's Arkansas may be contacted for all issues regarding client or donor 
privacy and your rights under Federal privacy standards. Requests to receive 
alternative communications, to inspect and copy, to amend and to an accounting, 
as well as any complaints must be in writing.

---

## Data Sharing

Information collected by Alzheimer's Arkansas is **not sold or shared with 
third parties** for any purpose.

If you do not want Alzheimer's Arkansas to contact you, notify Alzheimer's 
Arkansas and indicate that you do not wish to be contacted.

---

## Your Rights With Respect to Your Information

- You have the right to a separate paper copy of this notice at any time.
- You may obtain a copy of the current version of this Privacy Policy upon 
  request or on our website at www.alzark.org.
- You have the right to express complaints to Alzheimer's Arkansas if you 
  or your representative believe that your privacy rights have been violated. 
  You will not be retaliated against in any way for filing a complaint.
- You may request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures of your information.
- You have the right to request that your protected information be provided 
  by alternative means or at alternative locations.
